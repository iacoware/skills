# Roadmap workflows

The four session shapes the skill runs, as a map of where each one is written down. Nothing here is
normative: [`ROADMAP-GOAL.md`](./ROADMAP-GOAL.md) is the authority on intent,
[`skills/roadmap/SKILL.md`](../../skills/roadmap/SKILL.md) on behaviour, and the worked examples with
their verdicts live in [`evals/roadmap/recipe-app/SCENARIOS.md`](../../evals/roadmap/recipe-app/SCENARIOS.md),
against frozen starting states.

**Drawing from nothing.** A goal is declared and no map stands against it, so the full ceremony runs
and the map is written unasked. It does not end when the files are down: a first map is a draft to
argue with, and the argument is the first round of revision, in the same session.
*`SKILL.md` § 3 and `references/drawing-the-map.md`; scenario 0.*

**Re-truing.** Every session after. The author arrives with a situation and never a verb — *that one
is finished, the numbers are nothing like we assumed, and here is something nobody had thought of* —
and close-out, promotion, admission, revision and retirement are derived from it, close-out first.
*`SKILL.md` § 4; the three router scenarios, which are the readings hardest to get right.*

**Redrawing.** A new goal contradicts the recorded one, so the map is drawn again with what the
previous goal left behind entering as input: the archive, the id high-water mark, the exclusions, the
concerns, the candidates and the rows still open. Not a separate mode, and no reconciliation logic.
*`references/drawing-the-map.md`, "A redraw is this branch with more input"; `fixtures/redrawn/`.*

**Handing over a ready row.** The skill marks the row ready, derives the `triage` label without
storing it, and suggests the next step — the clarifying conversation for a slice, `/prototype` or
`/wayfinder` for a spike. It hands over and stops; it does not drive the chain below.
*`SKILL.md` § 6.*
